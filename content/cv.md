## Curriculum Vitae

**Since 2024** Research Scientist at Dana-Farber Cancer Institute (Department of Data Science) and Harvard Medical School (Department of Systems Biology)

**2018–2024** Senior Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Harvard Medical School and Dana-Farber Cancer Institute.
Advisor: Prof. Sahand Hormoz (Systems Biology, HMS)

**2015–2018** Postdoctoral Research Fellow at Harvard Medical School and Brigham and Women's Hospital.
Advisor: Prof. Olivier Pourquié (Genetics, HMS)

**2011–2015** PhD candidate on the Wellcome Trust PhD programme in Developmental Biology, University of Cambridge.
Supervisors: Prof. Alfonso Martinez-Arias (Genetics, University of Cambridge), Dr. Emma Rawlins (Pathology, University of Cambridge and Gurdon Institute)

**2010–2011** MPhil in Developmental Biology, University of Cambridge

**2008–2010** MSc in Molecular and Cellular Biology (magna cum laude), University of Heidelberg.
Thesis supervisor: Prof. Aurelio Teleman (DKFZ)

**2005–2008** BSc in Biology, University of Heidelberg.
Thesis supervisor: Prof. Michael Boutros (DKFZ)

## Service

**Since 2026** Co-organizer, [International Tea Room](https://tearoomseminars.github.io/), a monthly virtual seminar series on embryos and stem cell-based embryo models, particularly gastruloids. Labs present ongoing and unpublished work to a community of about 150 members from more than 60 labs; sessions run on the second Monday of each month, and early-career researchers are encouraged to nominate themselves to speak.

Ad hoc reviewer for *Cell Stem Cell*, *Development*, *Nature*, *PLoS Genetics*, *PNAS*, *Science* and others

Member of the International Society for Stem Cell Research (ISSCR) and the British Society for Developmental Biology (BSDB)
